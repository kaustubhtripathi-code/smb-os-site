What compliance actually requires

GST invoice generator, without the guesswork.

A valid GST tax invoice in India isn’t optional — it needs your GSTIN, the buyer’s GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes, the place of supply, and the tax broken out correctly. Intra-state supply splits into CGST + SGST; inter-state uses IGST. Get the split wrong and you risk input-credit rejections and penalties (a filing error averages ₹50,000).

SMB OS does the compliance for you: it validates your GSTIN with the official checksum, auto-splits GST by place of supply, tracks TDS, and generates the e-Invoice IRN with a signed QR. Is this GST invoice generator free?

Yes — 10 GST-compliant invoices free every month, no card needed. The Pro plan (₹299/mo) adds unlimited invoices, e-Invoice/IRN, history synced across devices and removes branding.

Does it generate the e-Invoice IRN?

Yes. Once your seller GSTIN is valid, SMB OS generates the Invoice Reference Number (IRN) and a signed QR. Production submission to the IRP requires your GSP credentials.

CGST + SGST or IGST — which do I use?

Intra-state supplies (buyer and seller in the same state) use CGST + SGST. Inter-state supplies use IGST. SMB OS picks the right split from the place of supply automatically.

Does it validate GSTIN and PAN?

Yes. GSTIN is validated with the official base-36 checksum and PAN by format, with inline errors so you never send an invalid number.
