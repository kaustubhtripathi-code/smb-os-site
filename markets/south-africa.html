SARS tax invoice, without the guesswork.

FAQ

Questions people ask before their first invoice.

What must a SARS tax invoice include?

It must be labelled “Tax Invoice” and show the supplier name and VAT number, recipient details, date, a description, and VAT at 15%. SMB OS includes all of these.

When must I register for VAT in South Africa?

VAT registration is mandatory above ZAR 1 million turnover. Below that it’s voluntary. SMB OS handles both registered and non-registered invoices.

Does it validate my VAT number?

Yes — the 10-digit SARS VAT number (starting with 4) is format-checked as you type.

How do clients pay?

EFT bank details go on the invoice (the most common B2B method), and PayFast covers cards, Instant EFT and SnapScan.
