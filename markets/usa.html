FAQ

Questions people ask before their first invoice.

When do I need to file a 1099-NEC?

You must file a 1099-NEC for any contractor you pay $600 or more in a calendar year, and collect a W-9 before the first payment. SMB OS auto-flags contractors at $600 and tracks W-9 status.

Does it handle sales tax?

Yes. Choose the seller state and per-state sales tax is applied to taxable line items (most freelance services are exempt).

Can clients pay by card or ACH?

Yes — generate (or paste) a Stripe payment link and a scan-to-pay QR appears on the invoice; Stripe covers cards, ACH, Apple Pay and Link.

Does it estimate my self-employment tax?

Yes. It shows a running SE-tax estimate (15.3% on 92.35% of net earnings) so quarterly estimated taxes don’t surprise you.

Can I export for QuickBooks or Xero?

Yes — export a clean CSV (RFC-4180, so commas and quotes in descriptions never corrupt the import).

Is it free?

Yes — 10 invoices free every month. Pro ($9/mo) adds unlimited invoices, history synced across devices, status tracking and removes branding.
