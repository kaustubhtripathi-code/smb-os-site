What compliance actually requires

ETIMS VAT invoice, without the guesswork.

Since 2024, eTIMS applies to all businesses in Kenya, regardless of turnover, and your KRA PIN must appear on every tax invoice. A compliant VAT invoice needs the supplier and buyer details, VAT at 16%, and the KRA PIN — miss any of it and the invoice isn’t valid for input VAT.

SMB OS formats KRA-ready VAT invoices, validates the KRA PIN as you type, and puts the total, VAT and PIN exactly where they belong. Add your M-Pesa Paybill and till, and the payment instruction lands on the invoice — and because it’s offline-capable, you can invoice even when the network drops.

FAQ

Questions people ask before their first invoice.

Is eTIMS mandatory in Kenya?

Yes — since 2024 eTIMS applies to all businesses regardless of turnover, and your KRA PIN must appear on every tax invoice. SMB OS formats KRA-ready invoices with the PIN included.

Does it calculate 16% VAT correctly?

Yes. VAT is computed at 16% on the taxable base, with VAT-exempt line items handled separately.

Can I collect payment via M-Pesa?

Yes. Add your M-Pesa Paybill and account reference and the payment instruction appears on the invoice; Flutterwave covers cards and mobile money.

Does it work offline?

Yes. SMB OS is offline-capable — you can build and save invoices without a connection and they persist on your device.
