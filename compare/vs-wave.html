Fair is fair

When Wave is the right choice

  • You operate only in the US or Canada.
  • You want free bookkeeping/accounting bundled with invoicing.
  • Your clients pay by card or ACH and nothing else.

If that's you — genuinely, use Wave. The moment your billing crosses a border, that's where SMB OS starts.

FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

Isn't Wave completely free?

Yes — Wave's invoicing and accounting are free (they monetize payments and payroll). SMB OS's free tier is 10 invoices/month. If you're NA-only, Wave is a great deal; SMB OS earns its keep the moment GST, VAT, UPI or M-Pesa enter your billing life.

Can Wave send a GST or VAT invoice?

You can add custom fields and tax lines manually, but there's no CGST/SGST/IGST logic, GSTIN validation, e-Invoice IRN, KRA PIN or eTIMS formatting. In compliance terms a template isn't the same as native support.

Does SMB OS do accounting like Wave?

No — SMB OS is deliberately an invoicing tool, not a ledger. It exports clean CSV for QuickBooks/Xero, so it slots into whatever accounting you already use.
