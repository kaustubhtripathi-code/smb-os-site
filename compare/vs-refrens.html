SMB OS vs Refrens

Refrens is a solid India-native invoicing platform for freelancers. SMB OS matches the India depth — GST, IRN, UPI — and adds Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa and the US in the same account.

Refrens understands Indian freelancers well — GST invoicing, a freelancer network, and India-first workflows. If your entire billing life is domestic, it's a reasonable home.

SMB OS covers the same India ground — GST with the correct split, checksum-validated GSTIN, e-Invoice IRN, UPI QR, lakh/crore, six Indian languages — and then does what an India-only tool can't: the same account bills a Nairobi client with M-Pesa and VAT 16%, or a US client with 1099 tracking. Cross-border earning is exactly where Indian freelancing is heading; your invoicing tool should already be there.

FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

Both do GST — what's actually different?

For domestic Indian billing, honestly, both will serve you. The fork is cross-border: SMB OS gives the same account native VAT (Kenya/Nigeria/South Africa), US 1099 tracking, M-Pesa/Stripe rails and six Indian languages — so your first foreign client doesn't mean your second invoicing tool.

Does SMB OS have Refrens' networking features?

No — no marketplace or lead generation. SMB OS is a focused invoicing tool: compliance, payment on the invoice, and history that syncs across devices.

Which is cheaper?

Both are freemium. SMB OS Free is 10 invoices/month; Pro is ₹299/month for unlimited invoices, all five markets, e-Invoice IRN and sync — priced so one avoided GST filing error (₹50,000 average) pays for 14 years of it.
