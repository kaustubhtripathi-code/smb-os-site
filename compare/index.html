SMB OS vs Zoho Invoice

Zoho Invoice is a strong single-market tool — free, polished, deep in India. SMB OS is built for billing across markets: GST + VAT + 1099 natively, UPI/M-Pesa on the invoice, offline-first.

SMB OS vs Wave

Wave is excellent free invoicing + accounting for the US and Canada. SMB OS covers what Wave doesn't: GST/VAT compliance, UPI/M-Pesa payment rails, Indian languages, offline-first.

vs

SMB OS vs Refrens

Refrens is a solid India-native invoicing platform for freelancers. SMB OS matches the India depth — GST, IRN, UPI — and adds Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa and the US in the same account.
